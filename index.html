What we do

From a loose button to a whole new silhouette

01

Alterations

Hems, waists, sleeves and tapering. We reshape garments to fit you exactly, pinned on you in the shop so nothing is left to guesswork.

02

Repairs

Seams, tears, zips, buttons and linings, mended properly. If a piece is worth keeping, it is worth repairing well.

03

Reconstruction

Restyling and rebuilding garments completely: vintage finds, inherited pieces and ambitious ideas.

The fitting room

Tailoring, the way it should feel

A proper counter, a private fitting room with full-length mirrors, and a coffee at the bar while your tailor explains exactly what your garment needs. You agree the fit and the price before any work begins.

Find the shop

How it works

Three steps to a better fit

  1. Bring it in

    No booking needed. Bring the garment, and wear it if it's a fitting, any day we're open.

  2. Get pinned

    A tailor fits and pins the work on you, explains what's possible and gives you an exact price.

  3. Collect

    Most alterations are ready in 2 to 4 days. We'll call or text you as soon as it's ready.

The craft

Made by hand, checked twice

Machines do the stitching; judgement does the tailoring. Every piece is pressed, checked against the pinned fitting and finished the way we would want our own clothes finished.
