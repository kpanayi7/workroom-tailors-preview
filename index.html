What we do

From a loose button to a whole new silhouette

Alterations

How it works

Three steps to a better fit

1. Walk in

No booking, no forms. Bring the garment (wear it if it's a fitting), any day we're open.

2. Get pinned

A tailor fits and pins the work on you, explains what's possible, and gives you an exact price on the spot.

3. Collect in days

Most alterations are ready in 2–4 days. We'll call or text the moment it's ready.

The craft

Made by hand, measured by eye, checked twice

Machines do the stitching; judgement does the tailoring. Every piece that leaves the workroom is pressed, checked against the pinned fitting, and finished the way we'd want our own clothes finished.
