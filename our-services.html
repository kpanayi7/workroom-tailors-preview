From a new zip to a new silhouette

Three kinds of work leave the workroom: alterations that perfect the fit, repairs that save the garment, and reconstructions that reinvent it.

01

Alterations

Hems and sleeves shortened, waists taken in or let out, garments tapered and reshaped to your body. Every alteration is pinned on you first, so the fit is agreed before the first stitch.

02

Repairs

Split seams, torn linings, missing buttons and broken zips, mended so well you'll forget where the damage was. A favourite piece shouldn't be retired over a small repair.

03

Reconstruction

Vintage finds resized, inherited pieces reimagined, dated cuts modernised. Bring the idea and our tailors will tell you honestly what the garment can become.

Common questions

Before you bring it in

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk in with your garment during opening hours and a tailor will see you. If it's a fitting, wear the garment or bring the shoes you'll wear with it.
How long do alterations take?
Most everyday work is ready in 2 to 4 days, and repairs are often sooner. If you have a deadline, tell us when you drop off; urgent work is often possible.
Can you work with delicate or unusual fabrics?
Silk, beading, leather and knits are all assessed in front of you. We'll tell you honestly what can and can't be done, and never attempt anything that would risk the garment.
Do you alter wedding dresses?
Yes. Bridal work is fitted in our private fitting room and always begins with a consultation, so the timeline works back safely from your date.
What if the alteration isn't right?
You try everything on at collection, in the fitting room. If anything needs adjusting, we adjust it. The job isn't finished until the fit is right.
