From a new zip to a new silhouette

Three kinds of work leave the workroom: alterations that perfect the fit, repairs that save the garment, and reconstructions that reinvent it.

Alterations

The everyday magic: hems and sleeves shortened, waists taken in or let out, garments tapered and reshaped to your body. Every alteration is pinned on you first — the fit is agreed before the first stitch.

Repairs

Split seams, torn linings, missing buttons, broken zips — mended so well you'll forget which side it was on. Favourite clothes shouldn't retire over a ten-pound fix.

Reconstruction

Complete transformations: vintage finds resized for today, inherited pieces reimagined, dated cuts modernised. Bring the idea; our tailors will tell you honestly what the garment can become.

Common questions

Before you bring it in

Do I need an appointment?
No — never. Walk in with your garment during opening hours and a tailor will see you. If it's a fitting, wear the garment or bring the shoes you'll wear with it.
How long do alterations take?
Most everyday work is ready in 2–4 days, and repairs often sooner. Up against a deadline? Tell us when you drop off — urgent turnarounds are often possible.
Can you work with delicate or unusual fabrics?
Silks, beading, leather, knits — the tailors will assess the piece in front of you and tell you honestly what can and can't be done. Nothing is attempted that risks the garment.
Do you alter wedding dresses?
Yes — bridal is fitted in our private fitting room and always planned as a consultation first, so the timeline works back safely from your date.
What if the alteration isn't right?
You try everything on at collection, in the fitting room, in front of a mirror. If anything needs a tweak, it's adjusted — the job isn't finished until the fit is.
